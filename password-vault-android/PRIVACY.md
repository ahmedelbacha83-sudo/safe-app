# Privacy Policy for Vault

**Last updated: November 2026**

Vault stores your passwords **only on your device**, encrypted with a PIN
you choose (AES-256). We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your
data on any server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no
network requests made by the app.

- **Data storage:** all data is kept in the app's local storage on your phone.
- **Encryption:** your entries are encrypted before being saved; they can
  only be read with your PIN.
- **Backups:** exported backup files are created and stored by you, on
  devices and services you choose.
- **No third parties:** no data is shared with anyone.

If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your vault is permanently
deleted (restore from your exported backup if you made one).
